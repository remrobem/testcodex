## Enter the Wilderness Together

The Lenten Quiet Day invites us into intentional silence and reflection. The retreat includes guided meditations, time for journaling, and optional one-on-one conversations with spiritual directors.

Participants will enjoy a simple shared meal, outdoor prayer walks, and a closing Eucharist at sunset.

## Registration Details

The suggested donation is $25 to cover meals and resources. Scholarships are available—please email **formation@epiphanychurch.org** to inquire. Childcare is provided upon request.
