## A Night of Story and Song

We gather on the twelfth night of Christmas to remember the magi who followed the star to Bethlehem. The evening begins with evening prayer in the sanctuary before we share a Mediterranean-inspired meal in the parish hall.

- Blessing of homes with chalk and prayers
- Interactive storytelling for children
- Traditional king cake and warm cider

## What to Bring

Bring a dish to share if you are able and a story about a time you noticed God&apos;s light in your life. Volunteers are welcome to arrive early to help set tables and hang star lanterns.
